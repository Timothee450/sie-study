Chapter 3

Bonds

Lending to an issuer: par, coupon and maturity, current yield, price versus yield, Treasury, municipal and corporate issuers, credit ratings, and the risks bondholders take.

What you'll learn

  • 3.1Bond basics. A bond is a loan: issuer, par, coupon, maturity, the indenture, interest payments and current yield
  • 3.2Price and yield. Premium, par and discount, why rates and prices move oppositely, and yield to maturity and to call
  • 3.3Issuers and ratings. Corporate, Treasury, municipal and agency bonds, and investment grade versus high yield
  • 3.4Risks and suitability. Interest rate, credit, inflation, call and reinvestment risk, and how bonds compare with stocks
