  • Assume $100 par unless told otherwise
  • Rate is fixed at issuance and never changes

  • Move the price: the dividend doesn't budge

Price up, yield down

Try it · 5% preferred, $5 a year
Current yieldAnnual dividend ÷ market price
  • Discount (below par): yield > rate
  • Par: yield = rate
  • Premium (above par): yield < rate

Exam tipPrice and yield always move in opposite directions.

Exam tipCumulative: all arrears + this year before common gets a cent. Straight: just this year.

Participating: a share of the good years

Normal year · 5%
Record year · 12%
  • Always gets at least its stated rate
  • Can receive more when profits are strong
  • Helps the investor → higher price, lower yield

Callable: the issuer can take it back

$100 par, 5% preferred, callable at 102 after 10 years
Call premium"Callable at 102" = $102 for a $100 share
Call protectionNo call allowed during the first 10 years

Exam tipIssuers call when rates fall. You get your money back just when you can only reinvest it at lower rates: reinvestment risk.

Conversion value = ratio × common price
  • Preferred should trade at or above its conversion value
  • If it's below, traders buy, convert and sell: arbitrage
  • Lets preferred holders share in the company's growth
